Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Introduction

Daily AI Auto Publisher is a personal automation application designed to generate and publish professional content to a user's LinkedIn profile.

Information We Process

The application processes user-provided topics and post content. LinkedIn authentication credentials are managed through n8n.

How Information Is Used

Topic information may be sent to Groq AI to generate LinkedIn posts. Generated content is sent to LinkedIn only when the user runs or enables the publishing workflow.

Data Storage

Workflow data and Excel files are stored locally on the user's computer. Third-party services such as Groq and LinkedIn may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

This application uses LinkedIn APIs and Groq AI services. Users should review the privacy policies and terms of those services.
